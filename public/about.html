What makes it work

  • Prices never come from the model. Every quote is calculated from the price list by group size, hotel class and single supplement, so a number can’t be made up.
  • It closes the loop. The traveller sees a summary, confirms, and gets a reference number; the sales team is notified instantly.
  • Quote first, ask later. It answers with a price straight away and asks at most one question per reply.
  • Honest when it can’t. No invented discounts, no payments in chat, visa answers point to the official portal, and anything off-topic is politely declined.
  • Large groups handled properly. Groups of 10+ get a starting price and a personal quote from a consultant.

Tested on 25 real-world conversations
